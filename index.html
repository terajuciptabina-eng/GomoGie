Get the best GomoGie experience

A quick setup helps you open GomoGie like an app and receive notifications on your iPhone.

1
Add GomoGie to your Home Screen

In Safari, tap the Share icon, scroll down and choose Add to Home Screen. Keep “Open as Web App” enabled if shown, then tap Add.

2
Open GomoGie from its new icon

Return to your Home Screen and launch GomoGie using the icon you just added.

3
Create your account or sign in

Your GomoGie account, posts and notifications stay linked to your account.

4
Enable notifications

Open Notifications in GomoGie and tap Enable push. Allow notifications when iPhone asks.
