# GomoGie — Brand Identity

## Official identity
- **Name:** GomoGie
- **Tagline:** Connect. Share. Discover.
- **Logo:** Wordmark only. No separate icon, symbol, badge, play button or media-player motif.
- **Primary presentation:** The single word “GomoGie” on a clean light background.

## Master wordmark
- Vector master: [assets/brand/GomoGie-wordmark.svg](../assets/brand/GomoGie-wordmark.svg)
- Raster exports: PNG on white and transparent background.
- Exact casing: `GomoGie`.
- `Gomo`: deep navy/charcoal.
- `Gie`: teal.
- Typeface direction: bold, clean, modern sans-serif with rounded geometric character.
- Keep the wordmark as one continuous word with no space or separator.

## Colour tokens
| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Brand Navy | `#142030` | “Gomo”, primary text |
| Brand Teal | `#00AE9C` | “Gie”, accent |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Primary background |

These are initial brand tokens. UI contrast combinations must still be checked before production use.

## Usage rules
- Maintain generous clear space around the wordmark.
- Do not stretch, skew, rotate or add effects.
- No gradients, metallic effects, glow, shadows, 3D effects, outlines or decorative flourishes.
- Do not add a standalone “G” emblem or play/media icon.
- Use the wordmark alone in compact placements; do not force the tagline into small spaces.
- On dark backgrounds, use a separately reviewed high-contrast treatment rather than applying unapproved effects.

## Brand consistency
- The official product name is **GomoGie** only.
- Do not use “GomoGie Social” or another suffix as part of the official product name.
- The tagline is optional in compact placements and must retain its exact wording when used.
- Do not present unapproved icon concepts as the official logo.

## Status and remaining production work
The wordmark-only direction and initial colour tokens are selected. The SVG master and PNG exports are prepared. Final font selection/licensing, optical kerning review, accessible UI colour validation and trademark/domain clearance remain to be completed.
