# GomoGie — Brand Identity

## Official identity

- **Name:** GomoGie
- **Tagline:** Connect. Share. Discover.
- **Logo direction:** Wordmark only. No separate icon, symbol, badge, play button, media-player motif or descriptor.
- **Primary presentation:** The single word “GomoGie” on a clean, light background.

## Wordmark specification

- Use the exact casing: `GomoGie`.
- Set in a clean, modern, rounded geometric sans-serif typeface.
- Keep the wordmark as one continuous word with no space or separator.
- Use deep navy/charcoal for `Gomo` and teal for `Gie`.
- Keep letterforms simple, balanced and legible at small sizes.
- Avoid gradients, metallic effects, glow, shadows, 3D effects, outlines and decorative flourishes.
- Do not add a standalone “G” emblem or a play/media icon.

## Colour direction

- Primary dark: deep navy/charcoal.
- Accent: clean teal.
- Background: white or a very light neutral.
- Exact production colour values and accessible contrast combinations are to be confirmed in the design-token phase.

## Clear-space and use

- Maintain generous clear space around the wordmark.
- Do not stretch, skew, rotate or add effects to the logo.
- Use a single-colour dark version where colour reproduction is limited.
- On dark backgrounds, use an approved high-contrast wordmark treatment; do not improvise effects.
- Do not add the tagline to the compact wordmark unless the layout has sufficient space.

## Brand consistency

- The official product name is **GomoGie** only.
- Do not use “GomoGie Social” or another suffix as part of the logo or product name.
- The tagline is optional in compact placements but must retain its exact wording when used.
- Do not present unapproved icon concepts as the official logo.

## Status

The wordmark-only direction has been selected as the current brand direction. Final vector artwork, font licensing, exact colour tokens, favicon/app icon treatment and trademark clearance remain to be completed.
